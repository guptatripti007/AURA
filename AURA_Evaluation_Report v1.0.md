## 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

### What It Is (Plain Language)

**Self Learning AURA** is Self Learning's enterprise-grade **Agentic AI platform** — a software environment that lets organizations build, deploy, manage, and govern autonomous AI agents that can reason, plan, and execute multi-step business processes with minimal human intervention.

Think of it as a **"factory floor for AI agents"**: instead of building bots from scratch or running isolated AI experiments, enterprises plug into AURA's marketplace, pick pre-built agents for their industry, customize them, and connect them to existing systems (SAP, Salesforce, etc.). The platform also enforces governance rules so agents behave within policy.

**Problem it solves:** Enterprises struggle to take AI from proof-of-concept to production at scale, while simultaneously maintaining control, compliance, and measurable ROI. AURA targets this "AI experimentation to AI operations" chasm.
